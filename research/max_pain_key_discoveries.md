# Max Pain Research - Key Discoveries

**Quick reference for major insights from max pain hypothesis testing**

---

## Discovery #1: Bi-Modal Distribution (Nov 6, 2025)

**Finding**: Max pain is binary, not gravitational.

**Evidence**:
- 7 hits within 1% margin
- 0 hits in 1-2% range (GAP!)
- Only 1 hit in 2-3% range
- Everything else >3% away

**Implication**: Either max pain works perfectly (<1%) or it doesn't work at all (>3%). No middle ground.

**Trading Impact**: Don't expect partial effects. Either conditions are right for max pain to dominate, or other forces take over completely.

---

## Discovery #2: Symbol-Specific Behavior (Nov 6, 2025)

**Finding**: Wide variation in max pain effectiveness by symbol.

**Evidence**:
- DAL & AAL: 50% hit rate (most reliable)
- LUV, JBLU, ALK: 25-33% hit rate
- UAL: 0% hit rate (never hits at 1% margin)

**Implication**: Max pain is not universal. Some symbols respect it, others ignore it.

**Open Question**: What makes DAL/AAL different from UAL? Liquidity? Market maker behavior? OI distribution?

---

## Discovery #3: Max Pain Itself Moves (Nov 6, 2025)

**Finding**: In 3 of 7 hits, max pain value changed significantly during the week.

**Evidence**:
- ALK week 37: Max pain moved +9.52% (from $52.50 to $57.50)
- AAL week 39: Max pain moved -4.17% (from $12.00 to $11.50)
- Both price AND max pain converged to meet in the middle

**Implication**: "Convergence" might be driven by changing OI patterns (max pain adjusting) rather than price manipulation.

**Open Question**: Which is the driver - does price move max pain, or does max pain move price?

---

## Discovery #4: Two Types of Hits (Nov 6, 2025)

**Finding**: Hits fall into two distinct categories.

**Type A - Already Close** (4 cases):
- Started within ~1%, stayed there
- May be coincidence (price already at equilibrium)
- Examples: AAL week 37, DAL weeks 39 & 40

**Type B - True Convergence** (3 cases):
- Started >3% away, moved to hit
- Shows actual movement toward max pain
- Examples: ALK week 37, LUV week 37, AAL week 39

**Implication**: Should we exclude Type A and focus only on Type B to test true predictive power?

---

## Methodology Validation (Nov 6, 2025)

✅ **Confirmed**: Our test matches max pain theory exactly.

- Max pain calculated from contracts expiring THIS Friday
- We test if stock hits that price ON expiration Friday
- Airlines have weekly options every Friday
- Our view pairs Monday → Friday correctly
