# Trading Style Guide

This document describes Ben's trading approach and constraints to help provide relevant, actionable advice.

## Core Strategy

### Position Types
- **Almost exclusively buy options** (long calls/puts)
- **Do not sell options** (no covered calls, cash-secured puts, spreads, or naked positions)
- Strategy focuses on directional plays with defined risk

### Trading Style
- **Swing trading approach**: Hold positions for days to weeks (not day trading)
- **Profit target**: Take profits once options grow beyond 25% gain
- **Exit before expiration**: Close positions well before expiration date - never hold to expiration
- **Entry timing**: Buy options when expecting price appreciation
- **Exit discipline**: Lock in gains rather than letting winners run indefinitely

## Portfolio Constraints

### Capital Allocation
- **Position sizing**: Up to ~$500 per trade
- **Risk management**: Limited downside to premium paid per position

### Trading Platform
- **Robinhood** - all trades executed through Robinhood mobile/web app
- Considerations: Commission-free but limited options analytics compared to professional platforms

## Symbol Universe

### Active Tracking
- **Primary focus**: Symbols with stock price up to ~$160
- Rationale: Affordable options premiums, meaningful percentage moves
- Budget has grown significantly — more of the universe is tradeable now

### Passive Tracking
- Monitor many additional symbols across price ranges
- Broader watchlist for opportunity identification
- May take positions outside primary range if setup is compelling

## Implications for Trading Advice

When providing trade ideas or analysis:
- Focus on **buying opportunities** (calls for bullish, puts for bearish)
- Avoid suggesting selling strategies (spreads, covered calls, etc.)
- Consider **affordability**: Options should fit within ~$500 position size
- Emphasize **swing trade timeframes** (days to weeks, not day trades or LEAPS)
- Remember **25% profit target** as baseline exit strategy
- Prioritize symbols up to ~$160 for active consideration
- Account for **Robinhood's interface** (no complex order types, limited Greeks display)
